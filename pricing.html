Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Our Plans?

Which plan is right for me?

If you’re an individual teacher, Teacher Plus gives you everything you need for your own classrooms. Schools and departments get the best value from the School Plan, while larger institutions with specific requirements can request a Custom Plan.

What is included in the Teacher Plan?

One teacher account with up to five classrooms, the core A1–B1 curriculum library, ready-to-use lesson plans and answer keys, quizzes and unit assessments, and standard email support.

Which plan should a school choose?

The School Plan is built for institutions: unlimited teachers and classrooms, the full A1–C1 curriculum, progress tracking and reports, onboarding with teacher training, priority support and custom school branding.

Can an institution request a customized solution?

Yes. The Custom Plan includes everything in the School Plan plus tailored curriculum options, custom integrations and SSO, a dedicated success manager and flexible contract terms. Request a custom quote and we’ll design a package around your needs.

How can I learn more before choosing a plan?

Book a demo and our team will walk you through the plans for your context, or explore the free sample lessons to see the curriculum in action first. You can also contact our team with any questions — no commitment required.
